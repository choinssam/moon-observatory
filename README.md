## Night Sky Lab

A free astronomy simulator for classrooms. Watch the Moon change shape night after
night, turn the lunar surface in your hands, follow the path the Moon takes across
tonight's sky, and take in a whole month at a glance. The solar system, the
constellations of each season, the tides, and solar and lunar eclipses are all here.

**Open it → https://choinssam.github.io/moon-observatory/en/**

- Nothing to install, no account, nothing to pay.
- No student data is collected. There is no server and no login.
- Positions are computed with [astronomy-engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy);
  the lunar surface is built from NASA LRO data.
- Written for a primary science class, but secondary students and curious adults use it as it is.

You may use, copy and adapt it for teaching, including translating it, as long as you
credit the author, do not sell it, and share any changes under the same licence
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). If something is wrong or missing, the Send a message button inside
